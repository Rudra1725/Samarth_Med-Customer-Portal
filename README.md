## 📌 The Problem

Patients, caretakers, and rural healthcare clinics in and around Pandharpur frequently encounter difficulties when searching for urgent or specialized medicines during odd hours:

- **Emergency Availability:** Late-night access to critical injections, anti-snake venom, pediatric drops, and cold-chain biologicals is often fragmented.
- **Queue & Wait Times:** Traditional walk-ins face stock inquiries that delay immediate patient care.
- **Wholesale Fragmentation:** Local nursing homes and private practitioners need a dependable channel for bulk supplies, syringes, and clinical consumables with valid GST & Form 20B/21B compliance.

---

## ⚡ Solution & Core Features

- **24/7 Digital Prescription Desk:** Upload prescription slips, specify quantities, and select either local counter pickup at Isbavi or local home delivery.
- **Direct Dispatch via WhatsApp:** Generates pre-formatted drug requisition payloads routing instantly to the dispensing pharmacist desk (+91 98347 30014).
- **B2B Wholesale Portal:** Tailored intake desk for clinics, hospitals, and pharmacies with validation for Drug License and GST numbers.
- **Zero Bloat, Zero Dependencies:** Built with pure vanilla web technologies to guarantee near-instant loading even on weak 3G/4G mobile connections.
- **Statutory Regulatory Compliance:** Dedicated sections for the Drugs & Cosmetics Act (1940), Schedule H/H1 validation policies, cold-chain safety terms, and customer data privacy.
- **Verified Reputation:** Displays real, unedited Google Maps ratings and reviews (5.0 / 5.0 based on 51 reviews).
