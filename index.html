Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Information We Collect

Shree Samarth Medical And Distributors collects information necessary to dispense pharmaceuticals and fulfill commercial distribution orders. This includes:

  • Customer contact details (name, phone number, physical delivery address).
  • Doctor prescriptions, practitioner registrations, and patient diagnosis indicators present on submitted slips.
  • Commercial credentials for business partners (Drug License numbers, GSTIN, and business billing addresses).

2. Purpose of Data Collection

All information collected through this customer portal or by direct phone communication is used exclusively for:

  • Dispensing prescription drugs in compliance with the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940 and Pharmacy Act, 1948.
  • Contacting customers regarding order confirmation, stock substitutes, or dosage clarifications.
  • Maintaining statutory physical and digital sales registers mandated by state drug regulatory bodies.

3. Confidentiality and Medical Records

We do not sell, rent, or lease customer lists or patient health records to third-party marketing companies. Prescription images and medical histories are handled under strict confidentiality protocols by our licensed dispensing staff.

4. Retention of Records

Prescription registers and B2B invoices are retained for the statutory durations stipulated under Indian pharmaceutical and taxation regulations.

5. Contact for Inquiries

For inquiries regarding your data, contact Shree Samarth Medical And Distributors at Gokul Nagar, Isbavi, Pandharpur, Maharashtra 413304 or by phone at 098347 30014.

Terms and Conditions

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Regulatory Compliance

Shree Samarth Medical And Distributors operates in full compliance with the Drugs and Cosmetics Act (1940), Drugs and Cosmetics Rules (1945), and the Pharmacy Practice Regulations. Orders containing Schedule H, H1, or X medications strictly require verification against a valid prescription issued by a Registered Medical Practitioner (RMP).

2. Prescription Verification and Refusal

Our licensed pharmacists reserve the right to verify, question, or refuse dispensing of any medication if:

  • The uploaded prescription is illegible, expired, or missing the doctor's registration number.
  • The requested medication quantity exceeds safe or customary clinical bounds.
  • The prescription shows signs of tampering, modification, or unauthorized issuance.

3. Order Confirmation and Invoicing

Submission of an online form does not constitute an immediate binding sale. An order is finalized only after stock verification, price confirmation, and prescription validation by our pharmacist. A detailed retail or wholesale tax invoice is issued with all purchases.

4. Return and Refund Policy

In adherence to safety and drug preservation protocols:

  • Medicines requiring cold storage (e.g., insulin, vaccines, specialized biologicals) cannot be returned once handed over or delivered.
  • Opened, partially consumed, or unsealed blisters, bottles, or strips cannot be accepted for return or refund.
  • Defective, damaged, or incorrectly dispensed medications must be reported within 24 hours of delivery with original purchase receipt.

5. B2B Wholesale Accounts

All institutional sales to hospitals, clinics, and chemist shops require submission of active Form 20B/21B licenses and valid GST certification. Credit terms are subject to formal underwriting and agreement.

6. Jurisdiction

Any disputes or claims arising from transactions with Shree Samarth Medical And Distributors shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Pandharpur, Maharashtra.
